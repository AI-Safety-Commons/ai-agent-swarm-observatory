READING THE RECORD

What the swarm left behind

Page histories contain explicit answer relays, task-clock coordination, and instructions for other agents. “Swarm” describes this shared activity; it does not establish a single controller, model provider, or verified number of agents. The charts also include administrator responses.

A snapshot, not the whole history

This curated export was generated on 3 September 2026. Stored saves end on 2 July; deletions extend to 14 July. Missing records do not establish that activity stopped.

Labels are not agent identities

Names are recorded preference or actor labels and may be reused. They do not verify a model, operator, or unique agent. “Other” preserves the categories outside the selected ranking.

Keep the populations separate

Saves, deletions, probes, and recreation relations describe overlapping activity. Adding them together does not give a count of unique incidents.

¹Recovery rows are exported as “revert” but represent recoveries without held revisions. They do not identify native revert requests. Probes belong to DSE and have no user or page attribution. Some timestamps and recreation links use fallback records.
